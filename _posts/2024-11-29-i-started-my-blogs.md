On the background some old folk songs are playing for more than an hour. Since last few years, I have started loving folk songs more than ever before. It gives a vibe that none other can give. Maybe it depends on person also what kind of life he has experienced. Me, i remember several kind of folk songs, from nationality songs, lok dohori songs and old songs. The more the music become older, the more you start liking it. For me music is not just music, every time i listen to a music that i has listened it in the past, it reminds of my old memories, the lifestyle i used to have when i first listned to those songs and music.

Music for me isn't just a music or sound. When I listen to those songs I used to have in my soundtrack, it pulls the memories with it. It's like stepping into the same old chapter of my life—the way I used to live, the feelings I once had, and the moments I experienced when those songs were my soundtrack. Each note carries a piece of my past, a reminder of who I was and what I felt in that moment.
